Event Wristbands

Tyvek, Satin/Polyester, Vinyl & Thermal Wristbands

Four wristband materials, one goal: secure, tamper-evident access control that carries your branding in full colour or 1 colour. Choose the right fit for your event below.

01 · Paper

Tyvek Wristbands

Our most popular option for single and multi-day events. Tyvek wristbands are lightweight, tear-resistant and fitted with a one-time locking closure — once it's on, it can't come off without being destroyed.

  • Tamper-evident, non-reusable locking closure
  • Full colour or 1 colour custom branding
  • Cost-effective for high-volume events
  • Ideal for: festivals, day events, gate entry & ticketing

Available in dozens of stock colours, or fully custom printed.

02 · Fabric

Satin / Polyester Fabric Wristbands

A premium woven fabric wristband secured with a durable plastic snap-lock closure. Soft, comfortable and built to survive multi-day events, with a look and feel that suits VIP, corporate and festival crowds alike.

  • Full colour or 1 colour woven / printed branding
  • Comfortable, soft-touch and durable for multi-day wear
  • Premium finish for VIP, sponsor & corporate access
  • Ideal for: festivals, weddings, corporate functions
SATIN / POLYESTER FABRIC BAND

Woven or heat-transfer branding options available.

03 · Waterproof

Vinyl Wristbands

Waterproof and durable, vinyl wristbands feature an adjustable snap closure and stand up to water, sweat and sun — making them the go-to choice for pools, water parks and outdoor events that run over multiple days.

  • 100% waterproof — built for pools & outdoor conditions
  • Adjustable snap closure, reusable for multi-day access
  • Full colour or 1 colour custom branding
  • Ideal for: water parks, pool parties, multi-day outdoor events
VINYL WRISTBAND — SNAP LOCK

Adjustable sizing fits both adults and children.

04 · Print-on-Demand

Thermal Wristbands

Direct thermal wristbands print instantly at the point of entry — no ribbon or ink required. Perfect for events needing sequential numbering, barcodes or on-the-day ticket issuing at the gate.

  • Instant on-site printing — no ink or ribbon needed
  • Sequential numbering & barcode-ready
  • Tamper-evident locking closure
  • Ideal for: gate ticketing, walk-up sales, cashless systems

Compatible with standard direct thermal wristband printers.
