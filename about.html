About Pop N Lock Wristbands

Centurion-Made Event Security, Built to Last the Whole Event

We're a Gauteng-based supplier dedicated to one thing: giving event organisers wristbands, lanyards and signage they can trust from gate open to last call.

Our Story

Started in Centurion, Trusted at Events Across South Africa

Pop N Lock Wristbands was founded to solve a simple but critical problem for event organisers: access control that actually holds up. Based in Unit 14, 1283 Embankment Rd, Centurion, we supply Tyvek, Satin/Polyester fabric, Vinyl and Thermal wristbands, along with accreditation lanyards, branded party cups and Correx signage — all custom branded to your event.

Whether you're running a music festival, a corporate conference, a school sports day or a nightclub event, our products are chosen for one reason: they lock in, they don't come off, and they look sharp doing it.
